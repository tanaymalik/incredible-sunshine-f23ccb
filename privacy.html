Privacy Policy

How Vedtaara handles the information you share with us.

Overview

This policy describes the information handled when you visit Vedtaara’s website, contact us, or schedule a consultation. The site uses optional analytics and advertising tools only after you allow them through Cookie preferences.

Information We Collect

If you email us or book through Calendly, we receive the information you choose to provide for that conversation or appointment. This may include contact details, scheduling details, and information you choose to include in booking questions. This website does not have an on-site contact form.

How We Use Information

We use information you provide to reply to your inquiry, coordinate appointments, and provide the consultation you requested. Calendly processes information when you use its booking service; its own privacy terms apply to that service. You can also contact us directly at vedtaara05@gmail.com.

Website Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 helps us understand site visits and interactions. If you allow advertising measurement, the Meta Pixel sends page-view events to Meta. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as the page visited and browser or device details. You can change or withdraw your optional choice using the Cookie preferences link in the footer. Read Google's Privacy Policy, Meta's Privacy Policy, and Calendly's Privacy Notice for information about their services.

Your Preferences

Your optional analytics and advertising choices are saved in your browser so we can remember them. Optional tracking remains off until you choose to allow it. To change your choice, use Cookie preferences in the footer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vedtaara05@gmail.com.
