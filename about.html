Ridhima Sehgal

Ridhima has been dedicated to the field of spiritual guidance and metaphysical sciences since 2014. With over a decade of experience, she has helped individuals gain clarity, confidence and direction through ethical and personalized consultations.

Her expertise includes Vedic astrology, numerology, tarot card reading and crystal healing. She is also a trained EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique) practitioner.

Her Approach

Ridhima believes that every individual possesses the power to transform their life when equipped with the right awareness and guidance. Her consultations are rooted in compassion, authenticity and practical wisdom, focusing on empowering clients rather than creating dependency.

Over the years, she has guided numerous individuals in navigating challenges related to relationships, career, finances, health and personal growth — through a holistic approach that combines ancient wisdom with modern understanding.

“Astrology should help you understand your situation and make your own decisions — not create fear or dependency.”
