How to Prepare for Your Session

A little preparation helps the time go further. Please have the following ready when you book or before your session begins:

  • Date of birth — the foundation for a birth chart reading and for numerology.
  • Time of birth — as accurately as you can. The birth time shapes key parts of a Vedic chart, so let Ridhima know if you’re unsure of it.
  • Place of birth — the town or city where you were born.
  • Your questions — a few areas or decisions you’d like to focus on. Writing them down beforehand keeps the session centred on what matters to you.

For a numerology consultation, the numbers connected to your name and birth date are used, so have the name you use day to day in mind as well.
