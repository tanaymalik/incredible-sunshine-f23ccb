Consultations

Personalized Guidance for the Questions That Matter.

Every consultation with Ridhima draws on Vedic astrology, numerology and tarot — chosen to fit the questions you bring, not the other way around.

01 — Birth Chart Reading

Birth Chart Reading

Understand the key themes reflected in your natal chart and gain personalized insight into the areas of life that matter most to you. This reading focuses closely on your individual chart rather than a general overview.

Useful for: those who want a focused, detailed look at their own chart — a natural starting point if you’re new to consultations with Ridhima.

02 — Vedic Numerology

Vedic Numerology

Explore the significance of numbers connected to your life and gain another perspective on your personal patterns and direction. Numerology offers a complementary lens to astrology, drawing on the numbers tied to your name and birth date.

Useful for: a different angle on questions you may have already explored through astrology, or as a starting point on its own.

03 — Tarot Reading

Tarot Reading

Use tarot as a reflective tool to gain perspective and clarity around questions, choices and situations you’re currently navigating. Tarot works well for specific, present-moment questions rather than broad life overviews.

Useful for: a specific decision or situation you’d like a reflective, outside perspective on.

Also Offered

Alongside astrology, numerology and tarot, the practice also includes Crystal Healing and EFT (Emotional Freedom Techniques), offering different approaches to emotional wellbeing, self-awareness and inner growth.

Crystal Healing

A gentle energetic practice using the natural properties of crystals to support emotional balance, grounding, clarity and inner wellbeing. Each crystal is chosen with intention, based on individual emotional and energetic needs.

EFT — Emotional Freedom Techniques

A therapeutic technique involving tapping on specific points while using affirmations to work through emotional stress, limiting patterns and inner blocks, supporting greater emotional balance and self-awareness.
