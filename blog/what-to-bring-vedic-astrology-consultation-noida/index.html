What to Bring to a Vedic Astrology Consultation in Noida

A practical guide from Vedtaara for making your consultation more focused and useful.

4 min read · Noida & online

Booking an astrology consultation can feel easier when you know what information will be useful. For a Vedic birth chart discussion, the most helpful details are your date of birth, time of birth and place of birth. It also helps to bring a short list of the questions or decisions you would like to explore.

At Vedtaara, consultations with Ridhima Sehgal are personalised around the questions that matter to you. The aim is to discuss patterns and perspective, not to hand you a fixed prediction or make decisions on your behalf.

The three birth details to prepare

Date of birth. Share the date as accurately as you can. If you are unsure about the calendar format or have documents with a different recorded date, mention that before the session.

Time of birth. An accurate birth time helps when discussing a Vedic chart. Use the most reliable record you have, and be open if the time is approximate or unknown. Do not guess to make the form feel complete; uncertainty is useful context.

Place of birth. The town or city where you were born is generally enough to identify the location. If you were born in a smaller place, include the nearest city as an additional reference.

Bring questions, not a script

Write down a few topics you would like to discuss. They might relate to career direction, a relationship pattern or a transition you are considering. You do not need to know astrology terminology. A plain-language question such as “Why do I keep reaching the same point at work?” gives the conversation a clear starting point.

If several issues are on your mind, mark the one that feels most urgent. This helps keep the session focused and gives you room to ask follow-up questions.

What if your birth time is uncertain?

Say so. A consultation can still begin with what you know, but uncertainty in birth time may limit how confidently some chart details can be discussed. Ask the astrologer how that affects the scope of the reading before relying on a specific interpretation.

In-person or online in Noida

Vedtaara offers in-person appointments at I-Thum Tower B, 4th Floor, Unit 412, Sector 62, Noida, by appointment. Online consultations are also available. Confirm the format and appointment details when you get in touch; please do not assume the office accepts walk-ins.

You can read more about consultations, learn about Ridhima Sehgal, or contact Vedtaara to ask about booking.

A useful way to approach the session

Think of the meeting as a structured conversation. Share the context behind your question, listen for the patterns being discussed, and ask for clarification when something is unclear. Take notes if that helps you reflect later.

Astrology is a reflective practice, not a guarantee of what will happen. Use the discussion as one perspective among the factors you consider. For medical, legal or financial decisions, speak with a qualified professional in that field.
