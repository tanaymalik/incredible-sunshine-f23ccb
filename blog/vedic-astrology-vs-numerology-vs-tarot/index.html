Vedic Astrology, Numerology or Tarot: Which Consultation Fits Your Question?

A practical guide from Vedtaara for making your consultation more focused and useful.

4 min read · Noida & online

If you are looking at several types of readings, the names alone may not make the choice obvious. A useful starting point is the question you want to explore and the kind of conversation you are looking for.

Vedtaara offers personalised Vedic astrology, numerology and tarot consultations with Ridhima Sehgal. Each approach can provide a different reflective lens; none guarantees an outcome or makes decisions for you.

Choose Vedic astrology for a birth-chart perspective

A Vedic astrology consultation uses your birth details to discuss chart themes in relation to questions such as career direction, relationship patterns or a life transition. Prepare your birth date, time and place. If your time is uncertain, mention that when arranging the consultation.

This may be a fit if you want to discuss broader patterns or how a current question connects with your chart. It should not be treated as a fixed prediction of events.

Choose numerology to explore name and date-based themes

Vedtaara’s Vedic numerology consultation explores numbers connected to your birth date and name. It can offer a complementary way to reflect on themes and personal direction. If you are asking about name selection or correction, be clear about the exact name you use and what you hope to understand.

Ask what information is needed and how the discussion will be framed before you book. Avoid making legal or financial changes to your name based only on a reading.

Choose tarot for a focused present-moment question

A tarot consultation uses the cards as prompts for reflection on a specific situation or decision. It may suit you when you want to explore how you are experiencing a current question and consider another perspective.

Bring one clear topic rather than expecting the cards to provide certainty about another person or guarantee a future event. You can ask how the interpretation connects to your own context.

Still unsure? Start with the question

Try finishing this sentence: “The thing I most want to understand is…” If you want to discuss longer-term patterns through birth details, Vedic astrology may be the natural starting point. If your curiosity is centred on name and date-based themes, consider numerology. If you want to reflect on a specific current situation, tarot may feel more direct.

You can also contact Vedtaara before booking and describe what you are looking for. Read more about consultations, Ridhima Sehgal, or contact Vedtaara.

Consultations in Noida and online

In-person appointments are held at I-Thum Tower B, 4th Floor, Unit 412, Sector 62, Noida, by appointment. Online consultations are available as well. Confirm the format and details when getting in touch.

Whichever format you choose, treat the session as perspective for your own reflection. Astrology, numerology and tarot do not guarantee outcomes and are not substitutes for medical, legal or financial advice.
